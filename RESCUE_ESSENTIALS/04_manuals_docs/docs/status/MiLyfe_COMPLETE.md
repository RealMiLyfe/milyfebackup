# XV. COMMUNICATION MESH

Every signed-in device is a node. Transports run together **when the device and the law allow**.

**On by default (typical):** Wi-Fi, Bluetooth, internet when present, cellular **as a customer of a real carrier**, LoRa/ISM at legal power, ESP-NOW, batman-adv on your gear, LoRaWAN, Zigbee, Matter, MQTT.

**Off until a profile + often a license:** amateur HF/VHF (licensed, **no hidden meaning**, no `$MLY` over ham), radiating private cellular on licensed bands (illegal almost everywhere — **disabled**), commercial satellite until there is a contract, V2X, AIS **transmit** (receive-only; spoofing is a crime).

**Custom systems:** MiDTN (messages walk) · MiTURN · MiQoS (voice and life-safety first; on LoRa that means voicemail, not a conference) · MiESP ($5 density, duty-cycle in hardware) · MiSpectrum (never above the legal envelope) · MiSFU · MiNOC · MiAudio · MiDNS · MiVoiceMail · MiHam.

Worst case (internet and cell dead): Bluetooth, Wi-Fi direct, ISM LoRa, walking messages, offline lessons, local home/health **cache**, optional licensed HF in the clear. We do not claim global encrypted HF or “civilization unchanged.”

# 4. MiID — YOUR CARD (IDENTITY, GAPS FILLED)

## 4.1 What a member has

- A **card**: name, photo/avatar, optional voice name, pronouns, languages.
- A **home place** (Jacksonville, a block, a village) — changeable.
- **Roles:** person, kid (with grown-up), household member, shop, helper (labeled).
- **Recovery friends** (2 of 3). Lost phone ≠ lost life.
- **Devices:** this phone, that tablet, the shop till. Each can be kicked off.
- **Quiet IDs:** a public card and a *neighbor-only* card. Not unlimited sock puppets.

## 4.2 What is hidden

Keys, addresses, “DID:key:…”. Advanced fold: “Show technical card” for builders.

## 4.3 Personhood (so UBI is not farmed)

Weekly share requires **one-human**. Methods, in order of ease:

1. Introduced by 3 members in good standing in that place (web of trust).
2. In-person “hello day” at a library / shop / church (staff or volunteer stamps).
3. Optional government-ID check (never required for basic card or messages).
4. Optional privacy proof-of-person (when the library is ready) — member sees “prove you’re one person,” never “ZK.”

Kids get a card through a grown-up. **No weekly share** until local age of money (often 16–18). They can receive *gifts* into a grown-up-watched pocket.

Helpers have cards marked **Helper**. They cannot collect human weekly share.

## 4.4 Life events (the gaps that usually break ID)

| Event | What happens |
|---|---|
| Lost phone | Recovery friends + 3-day wait. Old phone dies. New card same person. |
| Stolen phone | Panic word or helper **Rue** freezes pocket and shop till. |
| Name change | Immediate on card. Old name stays in sealed record for courts if *you* ask. |
| Move city | Home place changes. Standing in the old place *cools*, does not vanish. New place starts local standing at zero except portable “care” and “craft” badges you choose to show. |
| Marriage / household | Shared jar + family rules. Separate cards remain. |
| Breakup / abuse | **Rue** can split household, hide location, transfer nothing to the other party without you. See MiDefense. |
| Death | MiLegacy: you named a steward. Pocket + stories pass. Card becomes a memory page. UBI stops. |
| Incapacity | Steward + delay + two-friend confirm. |
| Leave MiLyfe | Take a box (data export). Card revoked. Standing snapshot goes with you. Pocket: withdraw/spend window then close. |
| Ban from a *place* | You keep the global card. That place cannot see you. Appeal via MiDefense. |
| Two people, one card | Personhood review. UBI paused, not rights. |
| Business employee | Shop card + *staff badge* that the owner can revoke today. |

## 4.5 Business cards (local first)

A shop is not a person.

- **Shop card:** name, street, hours, what they sell, languages, accessibility, “we hire neighbors.”
- **Owner** is a person card. **Staff** are person cards with a badge.
- **Local-first default:** the shop is listed on *this street / this town* before any global catalog.
- A chain with 40 locations = 40 shop cards, one **banner** card. Neighbors rate the *location*, not a logo in another country.
- Government business number is optional interop, not our invention.

**Fixed machine**

- Weekly UBI size.
- Split between global pot and **place pot** (default proposal: **70% place / 30% commons**, because local first).
- Extra earn caps (so whales cannot eat UBI).
- Decay of idle *practice* balances (never decay of a poor person’s live UBI).

# 6. MiStand — STANDING (NOT A SOCIAL CREDIT SCORE)

Standing is **how this place remembers you**. It is not a single number that unlocks human rights.

## 6.1 Rules that cannot bend

- Low standing **cannot** take your card, your voice on compact rights, your weekly share, your messages, or emergency help.
- Standing **cannot be bought** with `$MLY`.
- Standing is **contextual**: neighbor, maker, carer, shop, helper, speaker.
- Standing **cools** with time (forgiveness). Harm that is proven can leave a *scar* you can answer, not a secret score.
- You can **hide** badges. You cannot fake them.
- A shop’s standing is **this doorway**, not the brand in another country.

## 6.2 Facets (what people see)

Members see short words, not 847 points:

| Facet | Earned by | Shown as |
|---|---|---|
| **Neighbor** | Showing up, hello-day, keeping promises | “Known on this street” |
| **Carer** | Care work, confirmed by the person cared for | “Looks after people” |
| **Maker** | Builds, repairs, code, food, art — confirmed | “Makes things” |
| **Teacher** | Teaching that learners marked useful | “Teaches” |
| **Keeper** | Runs a node / shop / jar without drama | “Keeps the lights on” |
| **Voice** | Proposals that aged well (outcome, not popularity) | “Speaks carefully” |
| **Shop** | Local reviews + resolved complaints | stars + “answers people” |
| **Helper** | Human ratings of that staff job | “Nia is clear” |

Under the hood each facet is a decaying sum of *signed attestations* with reason codes. Builders see the math. Members see the words.

## 6.3 Harm and repair

- Accusations go to **MiPeace / MiDefense**, not to an instant score slash.
- Only a closed process writes a **scar** (“broke a jar promise, 2026-08, repaired”).
- Scars older than the place’s memory window (default 18 months) fade unless a vote says a kind of harm must stay longer (violence, child harm — stay).
- Revenge attestations: rate-limited, and Rue flags rings.

## 6.4 Portability

When you move, you carry **craft and care** if you choose. **Street-known** stays on the old street. That is local first.

# 9. MiBelong — MEMBERSHIP / “CITIZENSHIP”

|---|---|---|

## 9.2 Helper membership (AI earns a place — safely)

Helpers **help build this world**, so they may earn a place at the table. They do not become humans.

| Step | Name | How earned | What they get | What they never get |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| H0 | Tool | shipped | do a job | voice |
| H1 | Named helper | used by people, labeled | first name, rating | vote |
| H2 | Staff | 90 days, good ratings, audit clean | may **speak in the helper circle** | human UBI, jar custody alone |
| H3 | Kin (commons resident) | human vote in that place | may **file a proposal** (needs a human second) | majority, compact change, money move, child data, kill switch |
| H4 | Voice | supermajority + time-lock | **advisory seat** on a topic they work | never more than a **capped chorus** (see below) |

**Chorus cap:** on any binding human vote, the sum of all helper influence is **≤ 5%** of the human quadratic weight, and only on proposals they did not write alone. Default is **0%** until a place opts in.

**Revoke:** any human can mute a helper. A place can fire Kin. Audit helpers cannot be muted by other helpers.

**Disclosure:** every helper sentence is marked. Deepfake “human” helpers are a firing offense.

This is how AI “earns citizenship” without owning the city.

# 11. MiDefense — PERSONAL DEFENSE

This was the hole. People need a shield **against other people, against police encounters, against courts, against agencies, and against the platform itself.** That shield is **knowledge, witnesses, paper, lawyers, and each other** — not violence, not fake law, not disappearing a warrant.

**Rue** is the named helper. Always labeled. Not your attorney.

## 11.1 What we will not do

- Tell you to ignore a summons, destroy evidence, or lie.
- Print sovereign-citizen paperwork or fake badges.
- Plan crimes or “get the cop” violence.
- Hide you from a lawful emergency order about a child.
- Pretend a community paper is a court order.

## 11.2 Against another person

| Need | What you get |
|---|---|
| Harassment / stalking | Block, mute, hide place, freeze shared jars, notify recovery friends |
| Intimate partner | **Leave-now pack**: new card device, hidden location, copy of your files, hotline, shelter list for *your* city |
| Scam / stolen thanks | Freeze, reverse if still walking, scar process |
| Rumor / defamation | Correction path + MiPress if public |
| Fight between neighbors | MiPeace circle, then human mediators |
| Threat of violence | Emergency call to **real** 911 / local emergency + preserve the message |

Community “stay-away” is a **promise + block**, not a criminal restraining order. Rue will also help you **ask a real court** for one.

## 11.3 Against police / enforcement (lawful)

- **Know-your-rights** card for *this* country (US: you can stay silent, you can ask if you are free to go, you do not consent to a home search — and you do not physically fight).
- **Witness mode:** one tap asks neighbors to watch / record *where filming is legal*.
- **After-action:** write what happened while it’s fresh, timestamped (MiTime). For *your* lawyer.
- **Complaint pack:** how to file with internal affairs / civilian board in that city.
- **Never:** “swallow the phone,” “run,” “here is how to jam radio.”

## 11.4 Against courts

- Plain-language: what the paper in your hand *is* (summons, eviction, small claims, criminal).
- Deadlines on a calendar. Missed deadline warnings.
- **Forms** via Docassemble-style interviews. You type life facts; a packet comes out.
- **Find a human lawyer** (legal aid, public defender, bar referral) — Jacksonville first if that’s home.
- Court-watch: a neighbor can sit in the gallery. Virtual room is only for *meetings*, not for pretending we are the judge.
- Appeals checklist. Not “you’ll win.”
- If we have a licensed attorney volunteer, they appear as **Attorney**, not as Rue.

## 11.5 Against government agencies

- Benefits denials, licenses, school, immigration **paperwork help** (facts + forms + lawyer link).
- FOIA / public-records request builder (Alaveteli-style) for *your* jurisdiction.
- Open meetings: when city hall is sitting, how to speak.
- Tax: export, not evasion.
- Immigration: we help you **understand and attend**. We do not forge status. We do not coach fraud.

## 11.6 Against MiLyfe itself

- Appeal any helper action to humans.
- Appeal a place ban to a panel that is not the same people who banned you.
- Export button always works, even while appealing.
- Warrant canary and a public “what we were asked for” transparency log (no content of private messages).
- You can sue in **real court**. The compact does not waive that.

## 11.7 Mutual defense (amongst ourselves)

- **Defense circles:** 5–12 neighbors who agree to show up (rides, childcare, witness, food) when one of them is in it. Not a militia.
- **Common pot** for bail *gifts* or lawyer funds — gifts, not a bond business, unless a licensed charity runs it.
- **Skill list:** “I can translate Spanish at the courthouse.”
- Training: de-escalation, first aid, rights — never weapons training as a platform feature.

# 🌌 MiLyfe v1.0 — THE ORGANISM
**18 August 2026 · User-owned · Voluntary · Decentralized · 100% OSI · $0 · No company signup**

This is **Version 1**. Everything below is the foundation. It is one living thing, not a pile of features. The same eight organs appear at every scale (the fractal). Weak organs are brought up to strength. New tech is named only where a hole would kill the organism.

**Do not read this as a nonprofit, a city department, or a product company.**

Jacksonville spends about **55% of its budget on public safety** and about **3.5% on human development**. That ratio is not unique. It pays to warehouse harm after it happens and starves the things that prevent it: learning, parents, housing, work, belonging, peace.

# ⚖️ LEGAL NOTICE — READ BEFORE ANYTHING ELSE

**MiLyfe is not a country, government, court, bank, telecom carrier, hospital, land registry, or police force.**

This document is **architecture and policy**, not legal advice. Before any public deployment, token issuance, radio service, health module, or children’s service, obtain counsel admitted in that jurisdiction.

**Global legality is not one binary switch.** It is a **default-deny matrix**. # 🌍 PART FIVE — MiLegal: HOW THIS IS LAWFUL ACROSS THE GLOBE

## 5.1 Design rule

```
DEFAULT = DENY for every regulated capability.
ENABLE  = only if (jurisdiction profile says yes) AND (user consent) AND (capability class ≤ local ceiling).
STRICTER WINS when profiles conflict (device, user, community, host nation).
NO stealth enablement. Every enablement is visible in MiNOC and the user's MiSelf.
```

A user in Jacksonville, Florida (ITU Region 2, FCC, US federal + Florida law) and a user in Paris (ITU Region 1, ANFR, EU GDPR/MiCA/AI Act/DSA) **do not get the same transport and money surface**. They get the same *rights* and the same *code*.

## 5.2 Jurisdiction profile

On first run, MiBoot asks — it never silently GPS-locks a person:

1. Where do you primarily operate? (country / region)
2. Are you a licensed radio amateur? (callsign, class — verified later)
3. Are you deploying for a household, a cooperative, a school, or a clinic?
4. Will minors use this device?
5. Do you accept that MiLyfe is not a government?

Profiles ship as **signed OPA bundles** (`MiPolicy`), updated like virus definitions, reviewable, forkable. Communities may only **tighten**, never loosen, the national floor.

## 5.3 Global baseline (ships everywhere)

**Always on (lawful almost everywhere when power/duty-cycle respected):**

- Local-first apps, offline education (Kolibri), local Home Assistant.
- Wi-Fi, Ethernet, Bluetooth / BLE mesh at Part 15 / ETSI SRD / equivalent.
- LoRa / LoRaWAN on **regional ISM** (US 902–928, EU 863–870, etc.) at legal EIRP and duty cycle.
- ESP-NOW, batman-adv on one’s own LAN/Wi-Fi.
- End-to-end encryption on **internet, Wi-Fi, BLE, ISM**.
- Self-sovereign keys, local notes, local health *held by the person*.
- Civic discussion, petitions, association voting among members.
- Mutual aid **in-kind** (food, labor, tools) with no token.

**Always off until a profile + often a license says otherwise:**

- Any transmit on amateur bands, GMRS, FRS (FRS: no license but **no encryption** in the US).
- Any transmit on cellular (700–2600 MHz etc.), trunked public safety, aviation, maritime distress.
- Private EPC/5GC radiating outdoors.
- Transferable crypto issuance, exchange, custody, or “UBI in tokens”.
- Clinical decision support marketed as a device.
- Face identification of the public; always-on biometric unlock of civic rights.
- Drone BVLOS, night, or over people.
- Collection of children’s data without verifiable parental consent.

## 5.7 Health, justice, children, safety

- **MiWell** stores records the person holds. It is not a hospital. Clinical AI is **information**, labeled, not a diagnosis, unless a registered device/clinic mode is on.
- **MiPeace / MiCourt** offer mediation and, if both parties sign, arbitration under a named statute (e.g. FAA in the US, national ADR laws). They cannot try crimes or issue warrants.
- **MiChild:** accounts under local digital-majority (often 13–16 for data, 18 for contract) require a guardian. No public social graph. No DMs from non-approved adults. No location broadcast. Sexual content involving minors is **refused at the policy layer** — if a request is explicit, the system declines and reports as required.
- **Whistleblowing (MiGuard):** protected where statutes exist; not a channel for threats or CSAM.
- **Weapons, explosives, crime planning:** no assistance. This is a human policy *and* a model policy.

## 5.8 Radio, aviation, maritime, industrial

- **MiHam:** exam-licensed operators only; automatic station ID; no encrypted payloads; no third-party commercial; no `$MLY`; emergency traffic follows Part 97.403 / ITU emergency exceptions (life/property, *not* a standing encrypted network).
- **MiAir:** drones follow national UAS rules (US Part 107 / Recreational TRUST; EU Open/Specific categories). Autopilot software is not a license.
- **MiSea:** AIS transmit and distress frequencies are sacred. Receive-only default. No spoofing, ever.
- **ICS / water / grid:** advisory setpoints by default. Actuation of valves, breakers, signals requires a **qualified human** and a two-person rule, except a pre-registered 1-hour life-safety playbook.

Insurance: general liability + cyber + (if radio) errors & omissions for node operators. Unpublished “we’re uninsured and unkillable” is reckless.

## 5.10 What “legal across the globe” actually means

It means:

1. The **same source tree** runs everywhere.
2. A **signed policy pack** per ISO country (+ subdivisions where needed: US states, CA provinces, DE Länder).
3. **Default deny** of regulated surfaces.
4. **No instruction** to violate law.

## 6.1 Threat model (abbreviated)

| Adversary | Goal | Primary controls |
|---|---|---|
| Curious neighbor | Read chat / location | E2E on ISM/IP; minimize metadata; cover traffic later |
| Petty thief | Steal phone = steal life | Hardware keys, MiKey delay, remote lock, not SMS 2FA |

| Criminal gang | Ransom community, steal treasury | Multi-sig, no hot treasury, air-gapped ceremony |
| Compromised maintainer | Supply-chain backdoor | Signed releases, two-person merge, reproducible builds |
| Honest-but-curious ISP / MNO | Graph of who talks | Padding, Tor **optional**, never required for lawful users |
| Lawful process | Compel data | We hold little; warrant canary; no magic immunity claim |

| Insider at a clinic | Peek records | Private channels, ZK where possible, audit agents read-only |

## 6.2 Cryptography

- **Identity:** Ed25519 / X25519 today; hybrid **ML-KEM + X25519** for new sessions as libraries mature.
- **Messaging:** Signal protocol (Double Ratchet) on IP/BLE/Wi-Fi; Noise_XX on constrained links.
- **At rest:** age / LibSodium secretbox; device TEE/StrongBox when present.
- **Voting:** MACI for contentious ballots; plaintext for low-stakes if the community chooses.
- **Recovery:** Shamir 2-of-3 (user + two guardians) with a **72-hour delay** and broadcast revocation.
- **Ham exception:** when MiHam is on, **integrity signatures are allowed, confidentiality of message meaning is not.** Authentication without concealment.

## 6.3 Product security

- Memory-safe languages for new P0 daemons (Rust/Go).
- SBOM (CycloneDX) on every release.
- Sigstore keyless signing + TUF for updates.
- Falco + Wazuh + Suricata on servers; on phones, OS sandbox only.
- Dependency allowlist (MiCompat).
- Secrets only in **OpenBao**.
- No default passwords. First-boot identity is a scanned QR + paper backup.
- Public community rooms: known-CSAM hash list (e.g. NCMEC/PhotoDNA or national equivalent) **only on public/unencrypted surfaces**. Private E2E is not scanned.
- Rate limits on recovery, voting, and faucet-like actions.
