# MiLyfe Platform — Video Demo Script

**Length:** 3-5 minutes  
**Tool:** OBS Studio or Loom  
**Resolution:** 1920x1080  
**Voice:** Casual, confident, showing not telling

---

## Intro (15 seconds)

*Show the milyfe.fun landing page*

"This is MiLyfe. A platform that gives communities the tools to self-govern, build shared wealth, and support each other — built over 11 years for $0. Let me show you what it does."

---

## Onboarding (30 seconds)

*Click Sign Up → show the onboarding flow*

"Sign up takes 30 seconds. No government ID. No credit check. Pick a username, agree to the community compact, and you're in. You immediately get your first $MLY credits."

---

## The Wallet (45 seconds)

*Navigate to /wallet*

"This is your Pocket — three pots. Spending for daily use, Savings that's protected, and Community that funds proposals. Every Saturday, universal basic income drops. Everyone gets $100 MLY. It circulates locally."

*Show the balance, the UBI celebration animation*

---

## Governance (45 seconds)

*Navigate to /governance*

"Anyone can propose anything. Right now we have three active proposals — a community garden, Spanish translations, and shade structures at bus stops. You vote directly. Quorum is required. Everything sunsets. Nothing is permanent."

*Click into a proposal detail page*

---

## Street / Marketplace (30 seconds)

*Navigate to /street*

"The marketplace runs on $MLY. Buy, sell, trade — food, services, rides, goods. Community quests let you earn by doing things the neighborhood needs."

---

## Mi AI (30 seconds)

*Navigate to /mi*

"Mi is your AI assistant. Send money, find resources, explain proposals — through conversation. Ask 'where can I get free food near me' and it searches real Jacksonville resources."

*Type a question, show the response*

---

## Safety (30 seconds)

*Navigate to /safety*

"Walk-with-me timer. If you don't arrive, your contacts are alerted. Leave-Now kills your session instantly for DV situations. Encrypted journal nobody can read."

---

## Learn (20 seconds)

*Navigate to /learn*

"35 free courses. Know your rights. Financial literacy. Reentry support. Works offline."

---

## Closing (15 seconds)

*Back to home*

"Open source. People-owned. A system that still works after anyone leaves. The code is public. The votes are public. If it isn't here, it isn't the proof."

*Show the GitHub link*

---

## Recording Tips

1. Use the dark theme — looks better on video
2. Use a calm pace — this isn't a sales pitch
3. Show real data (the seeded resources, proposals, wiki pages)
4. Don't script every word — the bullet points are enough
5. If you mess up, just keep going — authenticity > polish
6. Upload to YouTube, embed on mayor site blog page
