# MiLyfe — Community Services Design

**Date:** August 22, 2026  
**Status:** Design Phase — No Code  
**Governing Authority:** U.S. Constitution + MiLyfe Platform Constitution  
**Core Principle:** Free people voluntarily choosing to help each other. No permission needed. No filings. No corporate structure. No middlemen. No asking.

---

## The Framework

This is **voluntary mutual aid between consenting adults**, protected by:
- **1st Amendment** — Right to assemble, associate, and contract freely
- **9th Amendment** — Rights not enumerated are retained by the people
- **10th Amendment** — Powers not delegated are reserved to the people
- **14th Amendment** — Equal protection, liberty of contract

Just like Bitcoin didn't ask the SEC for permission to exist, MiLyfe doesn't ask any regulatory body for permission to let people help each other. The people freely chose this platform. The people govern it. The constitution protects it.

**What this means practically:**
- People pool money voluntarily → mutual aid, not "insurance"
- People share costs voluntarily → community sharing, not "banking"
- People create documents freely → self-help, not "practicing law"
- People lend to each other freely → private contracts, not "regulated lending"

If two adults consent to share costs, lend money, or help manage each other's affairs — that's freedom. That's the constitution. That's MiLyfe.

## 1. MiHealth — Community Health Sharing

### What It Is
People voluntarily pooling $MLY to cover each other's medical costs. No premiums. No denials. No bureaucracy. You help your neighbor, they help you.

### How It Works
- Members opt in and contribute monthly (whatever they can — $25, $50, $100 MLY)
- When someone gets sick or hurt, the community covers it from the pool
- Community votes on large claims (over $5,000)
- No deductibles, no copays, no networks — go to whoever you want
- All funds transparent on the dashboard
- Telehealth included for all members (contracted community doctors who believe in this)

### Tiers (Self-Selected)
| Level | Contribution | What You Get |
|-------|-------------|-------------|
| Seed | $25 MLY/mo | Telehealth + Rx discounts + emergency-only sharing |
| Root | $75 MLY/mo | Full cost sharing up to $10K/year |
| Tree | $150 MLY/mo | Full sharing up to $50K/year + dental + vision |
| Forest | $250 MLY/mo | Unlimited + family coverage + elder care |

### The Pool
- Transparent balance visible to ALL members at all times
- If pool runs low → community alert → members can top up or reduce claims
- If pool is healthy → excess rolls to reserve → community votes on what to do with surplus
- No one profits. No executives. No shareholders.

### Disputes
- Claim disputed? → 3-member community panel (Level 4+ standing) reviews
- Decision in 48 hours
- Appeal to full community vote if panel decision contested

## 2. MiCover — Community Risk Sharing

### What It Is
People voluntarily sharing risk. Your car gets hit, your apartment floods, you die unexpectedly — the community has your back. Not insurance. Mutual aid.

### Categories

#### Property Risk
- Fire, theft, flood, storm damage
- Member reports damage → uploads photos → community verifies → pool pays
- Neighbor attestation (2 people who can confirm the loss)

#### Vehicle Risk  
- Accidents, theft, breakdown
- Community covers repair/replacement from shared pool
- Good participants (no claims, active in community) pay less monthly
- At-fault accidents: community still helps, but member contributes 25% back over time

#### Life Transition
- When a member dies: immediate $5,000 to family for burial/transition
- If member elected higher coverage: up to $25,000
- No medical questions. No age discrimination. You're in the community, you're covered.
- Funded by: $5/month from every participating member

#### Business Protection
- Community businesses covered for: accidents at their location, product issues
- Pool funded by participating businesses ($25-75 MLY/month)
- Claims adjudicated by business community members

### Monthly Contributions
| What | Monthly | Maximum Coverage |
|------|---------|-----------------|
| Property | $20-50 MLY | $10,000 per event |
| Vehicle | $30-80 MLY | $15,000 per event |
| Life Transition | $5-15 MLY | $5,000 - $25,000 |
| Business | $25-75 MLY | $10,000 per event |

### How Claims Work
1. Member reports event (photos, description, date)
2. Two community members verify (neighbors, witnesses)
3. If under $2,000 → auto-approved from pool
4. If over $2,000 → community panel (3 people, 24-hour decision)
5. If over $10,000 → full community vote (72-hour window)
6. Payout in $MLY (spendable immediately at local businesses)

## 3. MiTrust — Wills, Trusts & Estate Tools

### What It Is
People helping each other plan for death, incapacity, and generational wealth transfer. No lawyers needed for basic documents (just like you don't need a lawyer to write a contract between friends).

### Tools (Self-Service)

#### Last Will
- Guided builder: who gets what, who raises your kids, who executes
- Two community witnesses sign (any Level 3+ member)
- Stored encrypted in MiVault
- Updated anytime (latest version = valid version)

#### Living Trust
- Avoid probate: assets transfer directly without court
- Builder: name trustee (community member or family), list assets, name beneficiaries
- Trustee manages assets if you become incapacitated
- Successor trustee if primary can't serve

#### Power of Attorney
- Financial POA: who handles your money if you can't
- Healthcare POA: who makes medical decisions if you can't
- Builder generates document, you + agent sign, 2 witnesses

#### Community Trustees
- Members can volunteer as trustees for others
- Must: be Level 5 standing, complete estate course, be bonded by community pool
- Actions are logged and visible to beneficiaries
- Quarterly reports to beneficiaries
- Removal by beneficiary petition (community votes)

#### Representative Payees
- For members receiving government benefits who need help managing funds
- Community members can serve (must complete training + pass background check via community)
- All transactions logged and visible to the beneficiary
- Regular accounting reports

### Key Principle
- **You own your documents.** MiLyfe stores them. You can export them anytime.
- **You choose your people.** Trustees, witnesses, executors — all YOUR choice.
- **Everything is revocable.** Change your mind anytime. Latest document governs.
- **Transparency:** If someone manages your money, every transaction is logged.

## 4. MiBank — Community Financial Circle

### What It Is
People lending to each other, saving together, and managing money collectively. The way communities did it for thousands of years before banks existed.

### Services

#### Savings Circles (Tandas/SuSu/Chit Funds)
How it works:
- 10 people agree to put in $50 MLY/week for 10 weeks
- Each week, one person gets the full $500
- By end: everyone contributed $500, everyone received $500
- Zero interest. Zero fees. Pure trust.
- Order decided by: random draw, or auction (bid lower amount to go earlier), or need-based (community decides)

#### Peer Lending
- Any member can lend to any other member
- Terms set freely between the two parties
- Platform records the agreement and tracks payments
- No interest required (but lender can set terms — it's their money)
- Maximum suggested: $1,000 MLY (community guideline, not enforced)
- Default handling: standing reduction + community mediation. No collections. No harassment.

#### Emergency Fund
- 1% of every $MLY transaction automatically goes to community emergency fund
- Members in crisis can request immediate help
- Emergency committee (3 Level 4+ members, rotating) approves in 4 hours
- Maximum: $2,000 per member per year
- Categories: eviction prevention, medical crisis, car breakdown, utility shutoff, funeral costs
- No repayment required (it's aid, not a loan)

#### Bill Splitting & Group Savings
- Split rent, utilities, groceries with roommates/family
- Group savings goals (vacation, equipment, events)
- Automatic recurring contributions from participants
- Visible to all participants (transparent)

#### Financial Coaching
- Community members who completed MiLearn financial courses can coach others
- Budget building, debt strategy, savings plans
- Earn $MLY for coaching sessions
- Peer-to-peer, no certifications required — it's advice between friends

## 5. MiCredit — Community Reputation & Building

### What It Is
The community's own way of knowing who's reliable — without FICO, without Equifax, without the credit bureau racket that profits from keeping poor people poor.

### Community Reputation Score (Internal Only)
Based on:
- On-time savings circle contributions
- Peer loan repayment history
- Bill split reliability
- $MLY transaction consistency
- Community standing level
- Time on platform

This score is:
- **Internal only** — never shared outside MiLyfe unless you choose
- **Transparent** — you can see exactly how it's calculated
- **Improvable** — always, no matter your history
- **Not punitive** — low score doesn't lock you out, just affects lending terms

### Credit Building Tools (For Traditional System)
For members who WANT to build traditional credit (to buy a house, get a car, etc.):
- Rent payment tracking (you document your history)
- Bill payment documentation
- Savings history documentation
- Template letters for disputing bureau errors
- Guides for each bureau's dispute process
- Predatory lender database (who to avoid in Jacksonville)
- Calculator: "What that payday loan ACTUALLY costs you"

### Predatory Lender Protection
- Community-maintained database of exploitative lenders
- True cost calculators (shows real APR on payday/title loans)
- Alternative options: emergency fund, peer lending, community sharing
- Report new predatory practices (community flags them)

## 6. Integration Across Systems

### How $MLY Flows
```
Daily UBI ($10)
  ↓
Monthly contributions (member chooses):
  → Health sharing: $25-250
  → Risk sharing: $20-80
  → Savings circle: $25-100
  → Emergency fund: 1% auto
  → Remaining: spend/save/lend/trade
```

### Standing Unlocks
| Level | What You Can Do |
|-------|----------------|
| 1 (Newcomer) | Join savings circles, request emergency aid, use document builder |
| 2 (Neighbor) | Participate in risk sharing, peer lending (as borrower), health sharing |
| 3 (Active) | Serve as document witness, lend to others, join adjudication panels |
| 4 (Builder) | Serve on emergency committee, claims adjuster, financial coach |
| 5 (Leader) | Serve as trustee, representative payee, health pool governor |

### Constitutional Rules (All Services)
1. **Voluntary** — opt in, opt out, anytime, no penalty
2. **Transparent** — all pool balances, all claims, all decisions are public
3. **Democratic** — major decisions by community vote
4. **Non-discriminatory** — no one excluded based on who they are
5. **Accountable** — misconduct = immediate Tier 4 violation
6. **Amendable** — community can change any rule by 67% vote
7. **Auditable** — annual community audit of all pools and accounts
8. **Portable** — you can export your history and leave anytime
