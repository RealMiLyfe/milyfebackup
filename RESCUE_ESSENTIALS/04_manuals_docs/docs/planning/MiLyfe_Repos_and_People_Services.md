## Part 2: Services for the People Society Forgot

These aren't afterthoughts. These are the REASON the platform exists. MiLyfe was built for the people the system abandoned.

---

### A. MiReentry — For the Formerly Incarcerated

**Who:** 600,000+ people released from US prisons annually. Can't get housing. Can't get jobs. Can't vote (in some states). Society treats them as disposable.

**What MiLyfe Provides:**

| Service | How |
|---------|-----|
| **Housing without background checks** | Housing board allows "no questions asked" listings. Community vouches replace credit checks. |
| **Jobs that don't discriminate** | MiLyfe business directory marks "second chance employers." Guild pays from day one — no application needed. |
| **ID rebuilding** | Step-by-step guide to getting ID, SS card, birth certificate. Document vault stores what you get. |
| **Benefits navigation** | What you qualify for, how to apply, who to call. Mi AI walks you through it. |
| **Record expungement guide** | FL eligibility checker. Template petitions. Deadline tracking. |
| **Mentor matching** | Connect with others who've been through it. Peer support. Not counselors — real people. |
| **$MLY from day one** | UBI starts immediately. Don't need a bank account. Spend at community businesses. Survive while rebuilding. |
| **Voting rights restoration** | FL-specific guide (Amendment 4). Check eligibility. Registration help. |
| **Savings circle for reentry** | Dedicated circles for reentry members. Build savings without a bank. |
| **No-judgment community** | Standing system doesn't penalize criminal history. Your past doesn't determine your level — your participation does. |

**Who:** People sleeping outside, in cars, in shelters, couch surfing, one paycheck from the street.

**What MiLyfe Provides:**

| Service | How |
|---------|-----|

| **Day storage** | Community businesses offer locker space for belongings during the day |

| **ID recovery** | Same as reentry — step by step to rebuild documents |

| **Shower/laundry map** | Where to get clean. Updated in real-time by community. |

## Part 3: How These Connect to the Platform

### Every service above runs on the same infrastructure:
- **$MLY** as the universal currency
- **Standing** as the trust metric
- **Constitution** as the governance framework
- **Transparency** for all pool balances and decisions
- **Community vote** for major changes
- **Mi AI** for navigation and form assistance
- **MiConnect** for peer support and communication
- **MiVault** for secure document storage
- **MiLearn** for education paths specific to each population

### Entry Points by Need:
```
Person arrives at MiLyfe
  → "I just got out of prison" → MiReentry flow
  → "I'm sleeping outside" → MiShelter flow
  → "I'm fleeing abuse" → MiSafety flow (anonymous mode activates)
  → "I'm aging and alone" → MiElders flow
  → "I just aged out of foster care" → MiYouth flow
  → "I'm in recovery" → MiRecovery flow
  → "I served and I'm struggling" → MiVeterans flow
  → "I have a disability" → MiAccess flow
  → "I'm an immigrant" → MiImmigrant flow (language selector)
  → "I'm a single parent drowning" → MiParents flow
  → "I'm fine, I want to help" → Volunteer/Guild/Mentor flow
```

### People Who Help = People Who Were Helped
The person who was homeless last year becomes the mentor for someone homeless this year. The person who survived DV becomes the safety guide. The person in recovery sponsors the newcomer. That's the cycle. That's the community.

## Part 4: Repos Specific to Underserved Services

These could be sub-routes in the main platform OR separate repos if they grow complex:

| Repo | Why Separate |
|------|-------------|
| `MiLyfe-Reentry` | Complex: criminal records, expungement, voting restoration — unique legal landscape |
| `MiLyfe-Shelter` | Complex: real-time shelter availability, outreach integration, different device constraints |
| `MiLyfe-Safety` | CRITICAL security: anonymous mode, encrypted escape plans, anti-forensics — needs security audit |
| `MiLyfe-Recovery` | Sensitive: sobriety data, relapse protocols, harm reduction — needs careful UX |
| `MiLyfe-Elders` | Different UX: simplified interface, large targets, voice-first, caregiver dashboards |
| `MiLyfe-Youth` | Different onboarding: no parent, no history, need hand-holding, matched mentors |
| `MiLyfe-Veterans` | Unique: VA system navigation, military terminology, benefit calculations |
| `MiLyfe-Access` | Technically complex: screen readers, switch access, voice nav, cognitive load reduction |
| `MiLyfe-Immigrant` | Multilingual: full i18n, legal sensitivity, ICE alert system, mixed-status family tools |
| `MiLyfe-Parents` | Integration-heavy: calendar, custody, childcare matching, school coordination |

**Alternatively:** Keep them all in the main `MiLyfe-Platform` repo as specialized onboarding flows and route groups (`/reentry`, `/shelter`, `/safety-mode`, `/recovery`, `/elders`, `/youth`, `/veterans`, `/access`, `/immigrant`, `/parents`). Only split into separate repos when they get too large to manage.
