# MiLyfe Design Laws (Non-Negotiable)

These 10 rules cannot be broken. They block release if violated.

1. **Five primary destinations; no module explosion.** Today, Pocket, Learn, Street, Voice — plus You. That's it.

2. **One account and profile; household/shop are layers.** No separate apps, no separate logins.

3. **Practice money can NEVER visually resemble live money.** Different colors, different badges, different everything. A user must never confuse practice with real.

4. **Helper messages are always labeled.** Mi is never anonymous. Every response shows it's from a helper, not a person.

5. **Mi cannot send, spend, publish, vote, or change safety settings alone.** Every consequential action requires a human decision.

6. **Every public item shows freshness and source.** "Updated 2 hours ago" + who verified it. No stale data without warning.

7. **Every sharing action shows audience before confirmation.** You always know who will see what you're about to do before you do it.

8. **Every serious action has an appeal or human review path.** No decision is final without a way to challenge it.

9. **Export works even during a dispute.** Your data is yours. Always. Even if there's conflict. Even if you're leaving.

10. **Accessibility blocks release.** Not aspirational. Mandatory. If it's not accessible, it doesn't ship.
