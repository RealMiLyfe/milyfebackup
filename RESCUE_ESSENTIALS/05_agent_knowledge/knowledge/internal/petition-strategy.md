- Cost: $0
- Where: Duval County Supervisor of Elections, 105 E Monroe St, Jacksonville
- Phone: (904) 255-8683
- You CAN be your own treasurer
- You CAN use your personal bank account as the campaign depository
- This takes 15 minutes to fill out and turn in

**NOTHING ELSE CAN HAPPEN LEGALLY UNTIL THIS IS FILED.**

After filing, the SOE gives you the official petition forms that people sign.

## THE PARTNERSHIP MODEL

You don't knock doors alone. Organizations collect FOR you because your platform serves their people.

### HOW IT WORKS

1. You provide: Official petition forms (from SOE), the story, the platform demo
2. They provide: Their existing foot traffic, congregation, membership, events
3. People sign: At events they're already attending, at places they already go
4. Forms return: Weekly collection from each partner location

### THE ASK TO EACH PARTNER

"We're not asking for money. We're not asking for an endorsement. We're asking you to keep a clipboard at your front desk. When people come through your door — people you already serve — they have the option to sign. That's it."

## TIER 1 PARTNERS: CHURCHES (40+ Congregations via ICARE)

**Why churches work:**
- Guaranteed weekly attendance (Sunday)
- Trust already established between congregation and leadership
- ICARE's 40+ member congregations = 40+ collection points
- Faith community already cares about the same issues (justice, poverty, prevention)
- Average congregation: 100-400 members, many registered voters

**The ask to ICARE leadership:**
"Your congregations have spent years doing restorative justice work. 97% of your youth never reoffended. You've proven prevention works. The MiLyfe platform is the same philosophy — prevention infrastructure instead of reaction. We need ballot access to bring this to the whole city. Would your member congregations be willing to have a petition clipboard available after services?"

**What each church does:**
- Petition forms available at a table after Sunday service
- Brief announcement by pastor: "One of our community members is running for mayor. If you'd like to sign his ballot petition, the forms are at the back table."
- Volunteer from the congregation collects completed forms weekly
- Campaign picks up from ICARE central location every Sunday evening

**Math:** 40 churches × 5 signatures per Sunday × 15 Sundays (Sep-Dec) = **3,000 signatures**

## TIER 5: DIGITAL → PHYSICAL BRIDGE

The MiLyfe platform has real users. The mayor site has visitors. Convert digital engagement to physical signatures.

**How:**
1. Platform notification: "The candidate needs YOUR signature for ballot access. Find the nearest collection point."
2. Mayor site petition page: Shows a MAP of all collection locations (churches, businesses, orgs)
3. QR code on every printed material → links to collection point map
4. Email list (Listmonk): "Here's where to sign this week"

**The key insight:** You cannot collect petition signatures digitally in Florida. They MUST be physical, on the official form, with a wet signature. But you CAN use digital to tell people WHERE to go sign.

## COLLECTION LOGISTICS

### Weekly Cycle
- **Monday-Saturday:** Signatures accumulate at partner locations
- **Sunday:** Volunteers collect from churches after services
- **Monday:** Campaign collects from all locations, counts, logs in campaign API
- **Tuesday:** Forms delivered to Supervisor of Elections for verification (batch)
- **Wednesday:** SOE reports verification results (rejected = doesn't count)
- **Ongoing:** Adjust strategy — if certain locations produce, double down. If not, pivot.

### Tracking (Campaign API)
The campaign API already has `/petition/status` tracking:
- collected (raw signatures collected)
- verified (SOE confirmed valid)
- remaining (target minus verified)
- daily_rate (pace tracking)
- projected_completion

Update daily. Ground Game agent monitors pace and flags if behind.

### Volunteer Petition Collectors
Each partner location ideally has ONE person responsible for:
- Keeping forms stocked
- Answering basic questions ("Who is this? Why should I sign?")
- Collecting completed forms and storing them safely
- Handing off to campaign weekly

**The training:** 5-minute conversation. "Here's the form. People fill in their name, address, date, and signature. They must be registered voters in Duval County. Don't pressure anyone. Just make it available."
