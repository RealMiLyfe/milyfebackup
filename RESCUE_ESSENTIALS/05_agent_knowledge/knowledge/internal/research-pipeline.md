# RESEARCH PIPELINE — Quality Control & Verification System

## THE RULE: NOTHING PUBLISHES WITHOUT VERIFICATION

No article, post, thread, or statement goes public until it passes through:
1. RESEARCH (gather raw data from primary sources)
2. VERIFY (confirm with at least 2 independent sources)
3. CITE (attach specific document/URL/case number)
4. REVIEW (Compliance Officer + Fact Checker clear it)
5. ONLY THEN → Content Producer formats for publishing

## VERIFICATION LEVELS

### LEVEL 1: PRIMARY SOURCE (Highest confidence)
- Official government documents (budget PDFs, audit reports, meeting minutes)
- Court filings (PACER, Duval Clerk of Courts dockets)
- Florida Division of Elections records
- U.S. Census data
- Published city ordinances (Legistar)
- Public records request responses

### LEVEL 2: VERIFIED SECONDARY (High confidence)
- Major investigative journalism (ProPublica, Times-Union, News4Jax I-TEAM)
- Academic research (peer-reviewed, named institution)
- Official organizational statements (ACLU filings, OIG reports)

### LEVEL 3: CORROBORATED REPORTING (Medium confidence)
- News reports confirmed by 2+ independent outlets
- Published data from established nonprofits (Vera Institute, Police Scorecard)
- Expert quotes attributed with name and title

### LEVEL 4: SINGLE-SOURCE CLAIM (Low confidence — DO NOT PUBLISH ALONE)
- One outlet reporting without corroboration
- Social media statements (even from officials)
- Anonymous tips

### LEVEL 5: UNVERIFIED (NEVER PUBLISH)
- Rumors, speculation, things that "seem" true
- Anything from a single anonymous source
- Anything that contradicts known primary source data

## THE VERIFICATION STAMP

Every piece of research gets this stamp before it can be used in any content:

```
CLAIM: [specific factual claim]
SOURCES:
  1. [Primary source — URL/document/case#]
  2. [Corroborating source]
  3. [Additional corroboration if available]
VERIFICATION LEVEL: [1-5]
VERIFIED BY: [which agent]
DATE VERIFIED: [date]
STATUS: [VERIFIED | NEEDS_CORROBORATION | CANNOT_VERIFY | CONTRADICTED]
SAFE TO PUBLISH: [YES | NO | WITH_CAVEATS]
CAVEATS: [any framing requirements]
```
