# Terminology Map — What We Say Now

The old platform (MiJaxx/MiCity) used different language. The new platform speaks differently. NEVER use the old terms in any context — content, demos, conversations, or prompts.

## DEAD → ALIVE

| NEVER SAY (Old) | SAY THIS (New) | Why |
|-----------------|----------------|-----|
| MiJaxx | MiLyfe (or "the platform") | Brand consolidated |
| Tokens / $MLY tokens | Shares or credits | Not crypto, not tradeable |
| Wallet | Pocket | Plain language |
| Liquid + Vault | Available + Reserved | Simpler mental model |
| Standing (5 dimensions: contribution, integrity, expertise, community, resilience) | Standing (8 facets: Neighbor, Carer, Maker, Teacher, Keeper, Voice, Shop, Helper) | Attestation-based, not numeric |
| Standing score (numeric) | Standing level (new/growing/active/established) | Qualitative not quantitative |
| Wave codes | (removed) | Invite mechanic removed |
| Quests | Learning paths / contributions | Not gamified |
| Guilds | Places | Geographic community, not gamer clan |
| Governance proposals (yes/no vote) | Voice proposals (5-stage pipeline) | Much more sophisticated |
| Marketplace | Street > Shops | Integrated into Street tab |
| Traffic stop recording | Safety > Leave-now / Recording | Part of broader safety system |
| Birthday Vault | (redesigned — weekly share + jars) | Economy works differently now |
| Founding Citizens | Members / Neighbors | Platform isn't a nation-state |
| cOS / Constitution OS | MiLyfe (the platform itself) | Brand simplified |
| More Perfect (slogan) | **We the People.** | Retired. The frame is government BY the people, not "a more perfect union." |

## BANNED WORDS (MiPlain Language)

Never use in any public-facing content:
- Blockchain
- Token
- DID (Decentralized Identifier)
- Wallet (use "Pocket")
- Node
- Agent (use "Helper")
- Smart contract
- Web3
- DAO
- Protocol (in public content — fine internally)
- Governance (use "Voice" or "your voice")
- Infrastructure (use "what gets built" or be specific)

## TERMS THAT STAYED (but mean something different)

| Term | Old Meaning | New Meaning |
|------|-------------|-------------|
| Standing | Numeric score you grind | Peer attestations that cool over time |
| $MLY | Liquid crypto-like token | Weekly share everyone gets equally |
| Mi | Keyword-response chatbot + Ollama | On-device helper with strict rails and specialized personas |
| Place | Not used | Geographic community with jurisdiction and law pack |
| Stage | Not used | Practice (credits) vs Live (real value) — hard boundary |
| Keeper | Not used | Time-limited community steward role (auditable, limited power) |

## WHEN TALKING TO THE PUBLIC

Don't say: "Our attestation-based reputation system with 8 facets and exponential time decay"
Say: "Your neighborhood knows you. The people you've helped remember. And that means something here."

Don't say: "Weekly UBI issuance of 100 $MLY to personhood-verified members"
Say: "Every week, you get your share. Same as everyone else. Just for being here and being real."

Don't say: "5-stage governance pipeline with liquid democracy delegation"
Say: "You have a voice. And if you trust someone else to use it for you on a topic, you can hand it to them — and take it back anytime."
