## TIER 1: THE INCUMBENT

### Donna Deegan (Democrat) — INCUMBENT

**Status:** Filed for re-election April 16, 2026  
**War Chest:** $1.4M+ in political committee (as of Jan 2026)  
**Polling:** 53% vs Duggan (33%), 53% vs Curry (31%) — Aug 2026  
**Approval:** 62% approve / 37% disapprove (June 2025)

**Background:**
- First female mayor of Jacksonville (elected May 2023)
- Former TV news anchor (Channel 12/First Coast News, 20+ years)
- Breast cancer survivor and advocate (3x)
- Founded the Donna Foundation (marathon running + cancer support)
- Won 2023 general with ~52% vs Daniel Davis (R)

**Record as Mayor (2023-present):**
- Proposed 2026-2027 budget: $2.03B general fund (balanced, zero from reserves second year)
- Capital Improvement: $586M proposed
- JEA controversy: Defended CEO Cavey against political attacks tied to Curry lobbying firm
- Seeking to be first Democrat to win second term in 40+ years
- Working with Republican supermajority council (mixed results)

**Strengths:**
- High name recognition (decades on TV)
- Strong approval with Democrats (78%) and independents (68%)
- Incumbency advantage
- Personal story (cancer survivor, empathy brand)
- Massive fundraising lead

**Vulnerabilities:**
- Only 47% of Republicans approve
- Generic Republican beats generic Democrat 42-37% in Aug 2025 poll
- Republican supermajority council creates legislative friction
- JEA drama could stick if Curry angles connect
- "What has actually changed in 3 years?" narrative
- High cost of living still an issue
- JSO budget still dominates (same 52% public safety spending)

**Contrast with MiLyfe:**
- She runs the system as-is. We're building an alternative to it.
- Her budget is $2B and the neighborhoods look the same. Our platform cost $0 and delivers direct value.
- She promises. We prove.

## THE STRATEGIC LANDSCAPE

### Key Dynamics:
1. **Republican field is fractured** — no consensus challenger. This HELPS Deegan (repeat of 2023 where Davis survived a bruising primary before losing to her)
2. **Generic R beats generic D** — but no actual Republican candidate comes close in head-to-head polling
3. **JEA is the defining issue** — Curry's lobbying firm, the board drama, who controls the utility = who controls billions
4. **MiLyfe enters as a third option** — not R, not D. The platform itself is the argument.

### Our Position:
- We are NOT competing in the R vs D frame
- We are the "neither of these people will change anything" candidate
- Every other candidate is a career politician, attorney, or insider
- We are the only one who BUILT something instead of PROMISING something
- The platform IS the proof. Nobody else has receipts.

### What We Don't Do:
- Never attack Deegan personally (she has a cancer survivor story — don't touch it)
- Never attack military service (Diamond)
- Never attack immigrant backgrounds (Salem)
- Only contrast on RESULTS: what have you built? What proof do you have? Where is the $0 solution?
