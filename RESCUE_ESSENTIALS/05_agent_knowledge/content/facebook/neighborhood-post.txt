Hey Jacksonville — real question.

When was the last time your neighborhood actually felt like a neighborhood?

Not a Facebook group. Not a Nextdoor argument. I mean people actually knowing each other. Helping each other. Looking out for each other.

The kind of place where if Ms. Johnson needs groceries carried up the stairs, three neighbors already handled it. Where if a kid needs help with homework, someone down the street is already there. Where if you don't come outside for a few days, somebody checks on you.

That used to be normal. It doesn't have to be gone forever.

I spent 11 years building something. A platform where being a good neighbor actually counts. Where you earn for helping. Where your kids build their future by contributing to their community. Where your voice matters on what happens on your street.

It's not a tech company. It's not a government program. It's not asking you to buy anything.

It's a lifestyle. Built on the Constitution. Pursuit of happiness — as something you can actually live.

Jacksonville is the first city. We go first. And what works here can work anywhere.

I registered to vote for the first time in my life this year. At 40. To put my own name on the 2027 mayoral ballot. After homelessness, after 30+ arrests, after eating from trash cans — I built something beautiful and I want to bring it to all of us.

If you're a Jacksonville person — parent, business owner, teacher, first responder, retiree, young person trying to figure it out — this is for you.
