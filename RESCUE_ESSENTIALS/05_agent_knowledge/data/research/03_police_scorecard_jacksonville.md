# Police Scorecard - Jacksonville, FL Research

## Date Accessed
2026-08-27

## Source
- **URL:** https://policescorecard.org/fl/sheriff/jacksonville-city-cnty
- **Status:** Fully accessible, data rendered on page
- **Organization:** Campaign Zero / Police Scorecard Project

## Overall Score
**Average for 4 Sections: 39%**

Scores range from 0-100% comparing counties with similar population. Higher scores = less policing spending, less force used, more accountability, fewer low-level arrests.

## Section Breakdown

### Police Funding: 44% (+2% trend)
- **Budget:** $529.49M for 920,508 residents = $575 per resident
- **Ranking:** More police funding per capita than 81% of departments
- **Misconduct Settlements:** $4M per year (2012-14) = $38,699 per 10k population
- **Source:** US Census Bureau

### Police Violence: 44%
- **Killings by Police:** 80 (2013-2025)
- **Racial Disparities:** Black person 2.6x as likely, Latinx person 1.2x as likely to be killed by police as White person
- **Data Period:** 2013-25

### Police Accountability: 16% (-12% trend)
- **Total Civilian Complaints:** 5,510 (2017-22)
- **Ruled in Favor of Civilians:** 7%
- **Use of Force Complaints:** 457 reported, 5% ruled in favor of civilians
- **Discrimination Complaints:** 50 reported, 0% ruled in favor of civilians
- **Criminal Misconduct:** No data available
- **Source:** Police Department self-reported

### Approach to Law Enforcement: 51% (-17% trend)
- **Total Arrests:** 206,145 (2013-2025)
- **Low Level Offense Arrests:** 14,260 = 8 per 1k residents (higher than 34% of depts)
- **Low Level Offenses:** 7% of all arrests
- **Homicides of Black Victims Unsolved:** 55%
- **Homicides of White Victims Unsolved:** 32%
- **Jail Incarceration Rate:** 3,454 avg daily population = 4 per 1k residents (more than 62% of sheriff's depts)
- **Source:** Uniform Crime Report, MAP/Supplementary Homicide Report

## Key Findings Summary
1. **80 killings by police** (2013-25) with significant racial disparities
2. **5,510 civilian complaints** with only 7% ruled in favor of civilians (2017-22)
3. **206,145 arrests** with 7% for low-level, non-violent offenses (2013-25)
4. **16% accountability score** is critically low (-12% declining trend)
5. **Racial disparity in homicide clearance:** 23 percentage point gap between Black and White victim cases solved
