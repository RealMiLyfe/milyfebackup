## Sources Found

### 1. 2024 JSO Annual Report
- **URL:** https://www.jaxsheriff.org/Sheriffs-Office/docs/2024-JSO-Annual-Report.aspx
- **Status:** Page exists; appears to be a web-based report (not a single downloadable PDF via simple curl)
- **Key Personnel:** Sheriff T.K. Waters, Chief Edwin Cayenne Jr. (Homeland Security Division)
- **Note:** May need browser-based access to view/download the embedded report

### 2. 2023 JSO Annual Report
- **URL:** https://www.jaxsheriff.org/Sheriffs-Office/docs/2023-JSO-Annual-Report.aspx
- **Status:** Available (prior year comparison)

### 3. 2024 JSO Open Data Report - End of Year (DOWNLOADED)
- **URL:** https://opendata.jaxsheriff.org/Doc/60
- **Local File:** jso_2024_open_data_report.pdf (2.3 MB)
- **Key Data:** Use of force reporting, personnel & professional standards data. Includes definitions of force thresholds and reporting criteria.

### 4. 2023 Open Data Report - End of Year
- **URL:** https://transparency.jaxsheriff.org/Doc/57
- **Status:** Available for download (prior year comparison)

### 5. JSO Transparency Portal
- **URL:** https://transparency.jaxsheriff.org/
- **Status:** Active portal with daily-updated data
- **Key Data Available:**
  - Homicide data (criminal): https://transparency.jaxsheriff.org/Homicide/Criminal/
  - Use of force reports
  - Personnel & professional standards
  - Open data reports (multiple years)

### 6. JSO Open Data Portal
- **URL:** https://opendata.jaxsheriff.org/
- **Available Documents:**
  - Doc/60 - 2024 Open Data Report (End of Year) [DOWNLOADED]
  - Doc/58 - 2024 JSO Community Survey Topline Findings
  - Doc/31 - 2020 Open Data Report
  - Doc/12 - Earlier Open Data Report

### 7. Calls for Service (Live Data)
- **URL:** https://callsforservice.jaxsheriff.org/
- **Also:** https://www.jaxsheriff.org/Resources/calls-for-service.aspx
- **Status:** Real-time data, refreshed automatically
- **Note:** Does not include sexual assaults or child abuse cases

### 8. 2024 Murder Statistics
- **URL:** https://www.jaxsheriff.org/News/2024-Murder-Numbers.aspx
- **Key Data:** Jacksonville recorded 59 murders in 2024, the fewest since at least 1995. Less than half the number reported in 2023.

### 9. 2024 Community Survey
- **URL:** https://www.jaxsheriff.org/News/Archived-News/2024-Community-Survey.aspx
- **Key Data:** 68%+ of respondents agree/somewhat agree JSO is handling its job well (up double digits from late 2022 survey)
