## Key Findings at a Glance

### Budget
- FY26-27 total city budget grew 1.1% (+$22.6M)
- JSO budget (via Police Scorecard): $529.49M = $575/resident
- Higher police funding per capita than 81% of similar departments

### Accountability (Critical)
- Police Scorecard overall: **39%** (poor)
- Accountability section: **16%** (critically low, declining)
- Only **7%** of 5,510 civilian complaints ruled in favor of civilians
- **0%** of discrimination complaints upheld
- Only **5%** of use of force complaints upheld

### Racial Disparities
- Black residents **2.6x** more likely to be killed by police than White residents
- **55%** of homicides with Black victims unsolved vs **32%** for White victims
- 23 percentage point gap in homicide clearance by race

### Crime Trends
- 59 murders in 2024 (lowest since 1995, half of 2023 total)
- 2026 on pace for even fewer (per News4Jax mid-year report)
